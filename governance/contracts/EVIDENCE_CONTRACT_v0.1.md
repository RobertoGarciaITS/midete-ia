# Evidence Contract v0.1

Evidence demonstrates that acceptance criteria were actually met.

## Evidence types
- REVIEW: documented human/agent review
- TEST: automated/manual test output
- VALIDATION: schema/lint/static validation
- DEMO: reproducible demonstration
- COMMERCIAL: user/payment/market validation
- DEPLOYMENT: deployed endpoint/resource proof
- SECURITY: scan/control evidence

## Required fields
evidence_id, work_item_id, type, source/artifact, result, timestamp/commit where available, limitations.

Claims without evidence remain UNVERIFIED.
